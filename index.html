1. AGRICULTURAL PLAIN (+2.40 dB)

Dry soil has a low dielectric constant (ε_r ≈ 4). After the Sep 16–18 rain event, water entered the top 5 cm of soil (ε_r ≈ 80), sharply boosting L-Band HH surface reflectivity across Konya, Aksaray, and Kulu farmlands.

2. LAKE TUZ SALT PAN (−0.62 dB)

In the center of the frame, flat Lake Tuz darkened because a thin film of rainwater over the salt crust created a smooth specular mirror that reflected microwave pulses away from the satellite.

3. URBAN CORNER-REFLECTOR CONTROL (~0 dB)

Dense urban buildings in Konya (+0.30 dB) and Aksaray (+0.49 dB) stayed invariant, proving that the +2.40 dB agricultural shift is physical soil moisture, not calibration drift.

01 · STUDY AREA & MICROWAVE PHYSICS SLIDE 02 / 07

WHY L-BAND SAR IN TÜRKIYE'S DROUGHT HEARTLAND?

1. KONYA BASIN & LAKE TUZ

• Türkiye's largest closed agricultural basin (38.4°N, 33.3°E, EPSG:32636) suffers from severe drought and groundwater depletion.

• Lake Tuz is an official international CEOS Cal/Val Landnet satellite calibration site.

2. CLOUD-PENETRATING L-BAND

• Optical satellites are blinded by clouds during autumn rainstorms.

• NISAR's active 24.4 cm L-band radar penetrates clouds day and night, directly sensing surface dielectric permittivity (ε_r) in the top 5 cm of soil.

3. DUAL RADAR SIGNATURE

• Wet Farmland (+dB): Rain raises soil ε_r from ~4 (dry) to ~15–25 (wet), boosting backscatter.

• Salt Pan Water Film (−dB): A thin water film on flat Lake Tuz acts as a specular mirror, reflecting pulses away.

02 · DATA & 3-STEP PROCESSING PIPELINE SLIDE 03 / 07

REPRODUCIBLE COLAB PIPELINE ON REAL NISAR HDF5 GRANULES

STEP 1 · ASF DAAC AUTO-QUERY

• Queries NASA ASF via asf_search for POINT(33.3 38.4), Track 172 Ascending.

• Extracts L2 GCOV layer: science/LSAR/GCOV/grids/frequencyA/HHHH.

• Clips 6 Sep & 18 Sep 2026 passes to exact common UTM 36N grid.

STEP 2 · 5×5 SPECKLE FILTER

• Applies a NaN-aware 5×5 spatial uniform filter in linear power before dB conversion.

• Suppresses multiplicative radar speckle noise while preserving agricultural parcel and shoreline boundaries.

STEP 3 · LOG-RATIO & ±3 dB MASK

• Converts power to decibels: dB = 10·log10(P).

• Computes additive log-ratio: ΔdB = dB_18Sep − dB_06Sep.

• Thresholds at ±3.0 dB (2× power shift) to map soil wetting vs. specular pooling.
